# Ground rules

## Honesty
- A gate you didn't earn undermines every level above it. Don't fake a pass.
- Log scores truthfully, including failed recalls and wrong predictions. The error log is the most useful thing you produce.
- Predictions are honest forecasts. Predict before you look.

## Fair use
- These are learning methods, not ways around assessment rules (no unauthorized aids, impersonation, or false credentials).
- Name your sources when you teach.

## Wellbeing
- Never trade sleep for study. All-night cramming is not a method.
- Work in blocks with breaks. For when to drop a level, see `placement-and-pacing.md`.
- Adjustments that help you focus (written schedules, working alongside someone, a quieter space, novelty) are legitimate.

## When a technique fails
Write down what you tried, the conditions, and your guess at why. Don't drop it silently.
