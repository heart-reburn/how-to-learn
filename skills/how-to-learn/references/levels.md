# Levels and pass criteria

Read the criteria for the learner's level before they study. A level is passed when the learner shows the evidence below, closed book, and scores about 80% on the teach-back check.

## What passing looks like at each level

| Level | Name | Pass criteria | Evidence to show |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recall | List the core facts from memory. Score at least 80% on a self-test of 10 cards or questions, answers written before checking. | The fact list and the scored self-test |
| 2 | Explain | Explain why each core idea works, in own words, with no jargon the learner can't unpack. A smart 12-year-old could follow it. | A recorded or written explanation and the gaps found in it |
| 3 | Use | Complete a real task unaided, then log what went right and wrong. | The finished task and an error log |
| 4 | Diagnose | Given a failed attempt or a flawed plan, find the cause and fix it. Spot a method that feels easy but isn't working. | A written diagnosis and the fix |
| 5 | Judge | Compare two methods or plans, pick one, and defend the choice with evidence and its limits. | A written comparison with reasons |
| 6 | Create | Design a plan, exercise set, or lesson for someone else at an earlier level, and improve it after seeing it used. | The design and notes on what changed |

## Which techniques to lean on at each level

All eight techniques are useful throughout. These are the ones that do the most work at each level.

| Level | Lean on | Typical practice |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Recall | Retrieval practice, spaced repetition | Flashcards answered before checking, blank-page dumps, a review schedule |
| 2 Explain | Elaboration, teach-back | "Why does this work?" in writing, linking each idea to something known, explaining to a 12-year-old |
| 3 Use | Retrieval practice, interleaving | Real tasks without help, mixed problem types in one session, an error log |
| 4 Diagnose | Metacognition, interleaving | Finding the cause of failed attempts, predicting scores and comparing, spotting methods that feel easy but don't work |
| 5 Judge | Elaboration, decision quality | Comparing two approaches with evidence and limits, cutting what doesn't serve the goal |
| 6 Create | Teach-back, decision quality | Designing a plan or exercise set for someone else, coaching them, improving it from what happened |

To master the learning methods themselves, make "learning how to learn" the subject and run the same ladder on it.

## If the learner does not pass

List the specific gaps. Send them back to study just those. Retest in a later session, not the same sitting.
